# Privacy Policy for Funimals

Last updated: May 2026

Funimals is a child-friendly educational game designed to help children learn about animals, puzzles, memory games, and basic math skills in a fun and safe environment.

## Information We Collect

Funimals may collect limited non-personal information such as:

- Player nickname
- Game scores
- Leaderboard rankings
- Basic analytics and crash reports

We do not collect sensitive personal information such as real names, addresses, phone numbers, or payment information.

## Leaderboard System

Players may choose a nickname to appear on the global leaderboard. Inappropriate or offensive nicknames may be filtered or removed.

## Advertising

Funimals uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect anonymous usage data in accordance with Google's advertising policies.

Learn more:
https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

## Children's Privacy

Funimals is designed for children and families. We aim to provide a safe experience and do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect stored game data and leaderboard information.

## Third-Party Services

Funimals may use the following third-party services:

- Google Firebase
- Google AdMob

## Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated in future versions of the application.

## Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, you may contact us at:

fatihstudios.app@gmail.com
